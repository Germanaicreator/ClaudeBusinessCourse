How it works

  1. Fireflies sends a webhook when a meeting transcript is ready.
  2. The app fetches the full transcript via the Fireflies GraphQL API.
  3. GPT-4.1 analyzes the transcript and writes a proposal email + consulting contract.
  4. Two Google Docs are created automatically and shared with your Drive.
