If your recruiting agency uses AI to screen CVs or rank candidates — the EU AI Act applies to YOU.

Most agency owners assume compliance is the AI vendor's problem. It's not. As the deployer, you are legally responsible.

That means telling candidates AI was used. Keeping a human who can override the AI. Logging how your tools are used.

The good news? It's mostly process, not technology. You don't need to rip out your ATS.

Agencies that get ahead of this now will have a real edge with clients asking about AI governance.
