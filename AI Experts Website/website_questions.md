# Website Questionnaire

Answer every question below. Don't worry about writing perfect copy — just answer honestly and in your own words. Claude Code will turn your answers into a complete, professional website.

---

## About You & Your Business

1. What is your business called?

2. What is your name and your role in the business?

3. In one or two sentences: what do you do, and for whom?

4. What problem do you solve for your clients?

5. What makes you different from others who offer a similar service?

6. What does a client's situation look like BEFORE working with you? What are they struggling with?

7. What does a client's situation look like AFTER working with you? What has changed?

8. Who is your ideal client? (industry, company size, role, location — be as specific as you can)

9. In which languages do you work with clients?

10. Do you work locally, nationally, or internationally?

---

## Your Offer & Process

11. What is the main service or product you want to sell through this website?

12. What is the single most important action you want a visitor to take on the website? (e.g. book a call, sign up, contact you, buy something)

13. Walk me through what happens when someone becomes a client — step by step, from first contact to delivered result.

14. How long does a typical project or engagement take?

15. Do you offer any kind of ongoing support, retainer, or subscription after the initial project? If yes, describe it briefly.

16. Do you offer a free first step (e.g. free consultation, free trial, free audit)? If yes, describe it.

---

## Proof & Credibility

17. List 3–6 past projects or client results. For each one, describe: what was the client's problem, what did you build or do, and what was the measurable result?

18. Do you have client reviews or testimonials? If yes, paste them here including the reviewer's name and when they were written.

19. What are your most impressive credentials, certifications, or areas of expertise?

20. Have you been featured anywhere (press, podcasts, speaking events, awards)? List them if yes.

21. Do you have a YouTube channel, podcast, newsletter, or other content platform? If yes, what is the URL and what is it about?

---

## Practical & Technical

22. Do you already have a logo? (yes/no — if yes, what is the filename?)

23. Do you have a photo of yourself you want to use on the website? (yes/no — if yes, what is the filename?)

24. Do you have a hero/background image in mind? (yes/no — if yes, what is the filename?)

25. Do you have a short welcome or explainer video on YouTube? If yes, paste the YouTube link.

26. What scheduling or booking tool do you use? (e.g. Calendly — if Calendly, paste your booking link)

27. What is your contact email address?

28. What are your social media profiles? (paste links for LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, or any others)

29. Do you need an Imprint page (required in Germany/Austria/Switzerland)? (yes/no)

30. Do you need a Privacy Policy page? (yes/no)

31. What color or visual style fits your brand? (e.g. "dark and premium", "clean and minimal", "bold and energetic" — or give a hex color code if you have one)

---

## Your Customers' Biggest Objections

32. What are the most common questions or concerns potential clients have before they decide to work with you? List as many as you can — these will become your FAQ section.

---

## Anything Else

33. Are there any competitors or websites you admire whose style or tone you'd like to be inspired by?

34. Is there anything specific you want on the website that hasn't been covered by the questions above?
