3. In one or two sentences: what do you do, and for whom?

   I build custom AI automations for recruiting agencies that want to fill more positions in less time. I handle everything from sourcing to follow-up — so recruiters can focus on the work only humans can do.

4. What problem do you solve for your clients?

   Recruiters spend a huge portion of their day on tasks that don't require their expertise — writing job ads, screening CVs, sending follow-up emails, updating the ATS, building candidate reports. It's repetitive, slow, and expensive. I automate those workflows end-to-end so the team can handle a higher volume of roles without hiring more staff.

5. What makes you different from others who offer a similar service?

   I only work with recruiting agencies — I'm not a generalist. I know the tools they use (Bullhorn, Teamtailor, Greenhouse), I know the workflows, and I've seen the same bottlenecks dozens of times. Clients don't have to explain their industry to me. I also deliver a working Version 1 within 5 business days — not a strategy deck, an actual automation.

6. What does a client's situation look like BEFORE working with you? What are they struggling with?

   Their recruiters are drowning in admin. A placement that should take two weeks takes four because the team is manually screening 200 CVs, writing individual outreach messages, chasing candidates for documents, and updating the CRM by hand. Good candidates fall through the cracks. The team is burned out doing work a machine could do.

7. What does a client's situation look like AFTER working with you? What has changed?

   CV screening happens automatically. Outreach is personalised and sent within minutes of a new role going live. Follow-ups never get forgotten. The ATS is always up to date. Recruiters spend their time on calls, relationships, and closing — not copy-pasting. Most clients see a 30–50% reduction in time-to-fill within the first month.

   Recruiting agencies with 3–30 consultants, working in permanent or executive placement. Typically in the DACH region or the UK. The founder or team lead is aware that their current process doesn't scale, but doesn't have the time or technical knowledge to fix it themselves.

## Proof & Credibility

17. List 3–6 past projects or client results.

    Project 1 — Automated CV Screener (Executive Search Agency, 12 consultants)
    Problem: The team spent 3–4 hours per role manually reading and ranking CVs from job boards.
    Solution: An automation that pulls incoming CVs, scores them against the role requirements using AI, and delivers a ranked shortlist directly into their ATS.
    Result: Time spent on CV screening reduced by 80%. Shortlists now arrive within minutes of applications closing.

    Project 2 — Personalised Outreach Engine (Permanent Placement Agency, 8 consultants)
    Problem: Outreach to passive candidates was generic and had a low response rate. Writing individual messages was too time-consuming to do at scale.
    Solution: A system that researches each candidate's LinkedIn profile, recent activity, and current role — then generates a personalised outreach message for the recruiter to review and send with one click.
    Result: Response rate on passive outreach increased from 11% to 34%.

    Project 3 — Candidate Follow-Up Automation (Tech Recruitment Agency, 5 consultants)
    Problem: Interview follow-ups were being missed or delayed, causing candidates to drop out or accept other offers.
    Solution: Automated follow-up sequences triggered by ATS status changes — personalised, timely, and requiring zero manual effort from the team.
    Result: Candidate dropout between first interview and offer stage dropped by 40%.

    Project 4 — Job Ad Generator (Multi-Sector Agency, 20 consultants)
    Problem: Writing job ads for every new role was eating 30–45 minutes per posting.
    Solution: An automation that takes the role brief and outputs a fully formatted, on-brand job ad ready to post — in under 60 seconds.
    Result: Ad creation time reduced from 45 minutes to under 2 minutes per role.
